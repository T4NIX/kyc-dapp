Simple KYC dApp

Connect your MetaMask wallet (on Sepolia Testnet) to interact with the KYC Registry.

Status: Not Connected

1. Register Your KYC Hash

Generate a hash of your KYC documents and submit it. This can be any unique string for this demo.

2. Verify KYC (Admin Only)

If you are the admin, you can verify a user's submitted KYC.

3. Check KYC Status

Enter an Ethereum address to check its KYC status.
